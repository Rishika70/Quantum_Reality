## Overview

This repository contains the LaTeX source for the paper titled **"Quantum Spirals and Multi-Reality Space-Time: The Emergence of Infinity in the Expanding Void."**

The paper proposes a novel theoretical framework exploring the emergence of infinity within quantum mechanics, focusing on:

- The duality of **nothingness** (infinite possibilities) and the **void** (expanding unobserved space-time).  
- The concept of **two infinite entanglements** dynamically interacting in multi-reality space-time.  
- The mathematical modeling of these interactions through an extended Schrödinger equation incorporating spatial curvature and dynamic entanglement terms.  
- Philosophical implications linking quantum mechanics with metaphysics and consciousness.
